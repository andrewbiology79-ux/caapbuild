Question Bank Android build foundation - package version 0.2.15

This ZIP is an incremental Android/Room build-harness package, not a completed Question Bank application.
MainActivity is still a placeholder TextView; no feature of the Master Checklist is implemented.

Verified in CI (run 18, package v0.2.14, non-cached): Room KSP generation, 7 unit tests (CanonicalSqlContractTest 5,
RoomSchemaParityTest 2, strict), debug APK assembly. 35 Room entities, 48 indices, 39 foreign keys, explicit WAL.
The APK has not been installed or inspected on a device.

Database architecture: one physical QuestionBank.db file; 35 ordinary Room entities, 2 FTS5 virtual tables,
17 canonical triggers (11 business + 6 FTS) and 46 CHECK-emulation triggers (23 tables x 2) installed by the callback.
48 CHECK clauses vs 46 triggers is reconciled (not a discrepancy).
