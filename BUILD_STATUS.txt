LATEX LINE-BREAK MARKER <br> (22 Nov 2026) - SOURCE EDIT ONLY, NOT COMPILED / NOT DEVICE-TESTED
- Requirement: a manual symbol that starts the following content on a new line, so step-by-step formula lines can sit one below another. Chosen marker: <br> (NOT backslash-n: it starts \neq, \nu, \nabla; NOT double backslash: it is used inside aligned/matrix).
- Rules: <br> may follow a formula, sit between formulas or sit inside a formula (the formula is split in two). Text without <br> renders exactly as before; a plain Enter behaves as before. Two <br> in a row leave one blank row. A <br> touching a $$block$$ formula is ignored (a block already has its own row). Spaces around <br> are dropped; trailing <br> adds no blank row.
- Implementation: new pure file ui/question/LatexLineBreaks.kt (tokenizer moved here unchanged, plus expandLineBreaks / buildInlineLines / buildRenderRuns). RichLatexText.kt renders one FlowRow per visual line inside a Column; scrolling, hidden scrollbars and unchanged font size are untouched.
